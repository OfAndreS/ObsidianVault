#### *Trigonométricas*

>[!quote] **Def**
>**Sen(x)**
>
>1. $$\frac d{dx}[\mathrm{sen}(x)]=\cos(x)$$
>---
>**Cos(x)**
>
>2. $$\frac d{dx}[\cos(x)]=-\operatorname{sen}(x)$$
>

>[!quote] **Def**
>**Tangente:**
>
> 1. $$\frac{d}{dx} (tg ) = sec²(x)$$
>---
>**Cotangente:**
>
> 2. $$\frac{d}{dx} (cotg ) = -cosec²(x)$$
>---
>**Secante:**
>
>3. $$\frac{d}{dx} (sec ) = sec(x) * tg(x)$$
>---  
>**Cossecante:**
>
>4. $$\frac{d}{dx} (cosec ) = -cosec(x) * cotg(x)$$

##### *Log / Exponenciais*

>[!quote] **Def**
>$e^x$ 
>1. $$\frac d{dx}(e^x)=e^x$$
>---
>$ln(x)$
>
>2. $$\frac d{dx}\ln(x)=\frac1x$$
>---
>$\log _a\left(x\right)$
>
>3. $$\frac d{dx}\log_a(x)=\frac1{\ln(a)\cdot x}$$
>---
>$a^x$
>
>4. $$\frac d{dx}(a^x)=\ln(a)\cdot a^x$$
>---
>$\sqrt{x}$
>
>5. $$\frac{d}{dx}\bigl(\sqrt{x}\bigr) = \frac1{2\sqrt{x}}$$
>---
>**Khan** [Derivadas](https://pt.khanacademy.org/math/differential-calculus/dc-diff-intro/dc-more-diff-rules/e/derivatives-of-ex-and-lnx) **Khan** [Derivadas](https://pt.khanacademy.org/math/ap-calculus-ab/ab-differentiation-2-new/ab-3-1b/e/differentiate-exponential-functions-intro?classCode=DFS8P8JM)**Conexões:** [[Euler & ln(x)]]
